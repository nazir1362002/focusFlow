Day 1 – Self Discovery

Understand where you are right now and why focus matters to you.

1. What is the main reason you want to improve your focus?

2. On a scale of 1–10, how would you rate your current focus level?

3. What is your biggest distraction right now?

4. What does “better focus” look like for you in 7 days?

5. How many hours per day do you currently spend on focused deep work?
